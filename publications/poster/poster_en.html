Building Your Own Local AI

Empower your organization's document search with AI

Ask anything about your accreditation documents, in everyday language. Every answer names the file and the page it came from, and you can open that document on the spot.

The problem

  • — Accreditation evidence is scattered across thousands of files on a shared drive: PDFs, Word, Excel, slides, even scanned pages.
  • — A single assessor question can take half an hour just to open the files one by one.

How it works

1
You type a question in everyday language.
2
The system finds the most relevant documents and summarises them for you.
3
It shows evidence cards: document name, page, and the quoted passage. One click opens or downloads the original.

Why you can trust the answers

  • ✓ Check it yourself. Every sentence carries a source number, and the document opens right away.
  • ✓ No making things up. Answers come only from the documents. If it isn't in the archive, the system says so.
  • ✓ Originals stay safe. The system only reads files on the shared drive; it never changes them.
  • ✓ Data stays in the office. Everything runs on the programme's own computer.

The technology behind it

The system uses RAG — Retrieval-Augmented Generation: the AI may only answer from the documents it finds. The AI model is interchangeable:

Local — Qwen3 8B on the programme's own computer; document text never leaves the office.
Cloud — ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude, when you want sharper answers.
